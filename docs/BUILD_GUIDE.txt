CHAPTER 1

Product Direction and Scope

1.1 What Aethon does
Aethon is a record and coordination platform built around a single person and the people who help them stay at
home. It follows that person across the stages of care they move through, and keeps one continuous record rather
than a series of disconnected ones.

The product pursues two objectives at once.

Objective one - independence
  •​ Extend the period during which a person can live independently at home.
  •​ Recognise early when that period is coming to an end, so support can be arranged before a crisis forces the
     decision.
  •​ Carry the record through the transition, so that moving to supported or residential care does not mean starting
     again from a blank page.

Objective two - the people providing care
  •​ Reduce the documentation burden on professional carers, so time goes to the person rather than to paperwork.
  •​ Give family members a reliable view of how their relative is doing, without telephoning the organisation.
  •​ Give a facility or home care service a live operational picture of who has been seen and what needs attention.

1.2 The three stages
A person typically passes through three stages. Most software serves exactly one of them, which is why records are
lost at every boundary. Aethon is designed so that the same record persists across all three.

 Stage                  Situation                                                    Who uses the product

 A - Independent        Living at home, assisted informally by family.               The person themselves, and family members.

 B - Supported          Living at home with a professional home care service         Adds the visiting carer and the service
                        visiting.                                                    coordinator.

 C - Residential        Living in a care facility.                                   Adds facility staff and management.

The record is the product. A person entering stage C arrives with a documented history of their medications, their
observed condition over time, their stated priorities and their care transitions. That continuity is the thing no
single-stage product can offer.

1.3 Scope of this build
This document specifies the minimum viable product. It covers stages A and B fully and lays the foundation for stage C.
The intent is a working system that a real home care service can use, not a demonstration.

 In scope                                                                Deliberately excluded

 Resident record with independence baseline and care stage               Any form of automated clinical assessment, risk score or
 history                                                                 recommendation

1.4 Design constraints
Four constraints apply throughout and are enforced in the build instructions rather than left to judgement.

 Constraint                       Rule

 Display, do not evaluate         The software presents observations and trends. It does not combine inputs to reach conclusions,
                                  does not apply thresholds that change what the user sees, and does not recommend action. If an
                                  instruction requires a comparison against a threshold to decide what to display, that instruction
                                  is wrong.

 Data stays close                 Voice audio is transcribed on the device and deleted immediately. Stored data resides in the
                                  European Union. No third-party processor handles health information in this build.

 Accessibility for older users    On any screen a resident sees: minimum 22pt text, minimum 80px tap targets, no swipe
                                  gestures, no hidden navigation, nothing that disappears on a timer.

 Every increment ships            Each increment in this document leaves the application installable and working. There is no state
                                  in which the build is half-finished for more than one working session.

3.5 Authentication and role routing

                                                                    11

 Prerequisites          3.2 and 3.4 complete                                Time                    Cost
                                                                            3 hours                 None
Implements passwordless sign-in and routes each user to the correct part of the application based on their stored role.
No password is ever created, stored or transmitted.

How it works
  24.​      The user enters an email address and requests a link.
  25.​      Supabase sends a single-use link of the form aethon://auth/callback with a token. It expires after one hour.
  26.​      Because the aethon URL scheme is registered, iOS opens the application rather than a browser.
  27.​      The application exchanges the token for a session, which persists in device storage.
  28.​      The application reads the user's role from user_profiles and renders the corresponding navigator.

Sign-in is invitation only. The signInWithOtp call sets shouldCreateUser to false, so only accounts an administrator has
already created can authenticate. Nobody can register themselves.

anyone stayed independent longer, and that record cannot be reconstructed retrospectively.

5.2 On-device transcription
 Prerequisites          5.1 complete                                              Time                         Cost
                                                                                  4 hours                      None
Converts the recording to text using a speech model bundled inside the application. The model is selected empirically
before any code is written, because the right size depends entirely on the speech of the people who will use it.

Select the model first
Obtain a recording of thirty to sixty seconds from a carer who will use the product, speaking as they would when
dictating a real note rather than in conversation. Test each model size against that recording and compare the result to
what was actually said.

 Model             Size when quantised        Typical time for a 30 second note          Use when

 tiny              Approximately 40 MB        A few seconds                              Speed matters far more than accuracy.
                                                                                         Usually too weak for regional speech.

 base              Approximately 60 MB        5 to 10 seconds                            The default starting point. Test this first.

 small             Approximately 180 MB       15 to 25 seconds                           Base is not accurate enough and the
                                                                                         background flow makes the wait invisible.

 medium            Approximately 500 MB       45 seconds or more                         Not recommended on a handset. The size
                                                                                         and battery cost outweigh the gain.

Record the transcript and the elapsed time for each size. Choose the smallest model whose output a carer would
accept with light correction. Then repeat the best result with the vocabulary prompt described in the build instruction
below, which biases the model towards clinical and medication terms and usually improves names noticeably.

If no model size produces usable output, stop before building. The options, in order: ask whether the carer will
dictate in standard German and retest; move up one model size and adopt the background flow; evaluate a regional
speech provider offering on-premise or in-country processing; or replace free speech with structured input, which is a
different product and should be planned as one.

Bundle the model
Download the chosen model file from the whisper.cpp model repository and add it to the Xcode project under a
models group with copy items enabled. For a small number of testers, bundling is simpler than downloading on first
launch and saves an hour of work; revisit that decision above roughly five testers.

Transcribe without making the carer wait
On-device transcription is slower than a network service. Rather than reducing the wait, remove it: save the note
immediately, transcribe in the background, and fill the text in when it completes. The carer taps stop, sees a
confirmation and leaves.

   const VOCABULARY =
   'Pflegedokumentation: Blutdruck, Puls, Medikamente, Mobilitaet, ' +

   'Aspirin, Klientin, Klient'

   let ctx: any = null

   // Load once and keep the context. Loading costs one to three seconds
   // and must not be repeated per note.
   export async function initTranscription() {
   if (ctx) return ctx
   ctx = await initWhisper({
   filePath: require('../../models/ggml-base-q5_1.bin'),
   })
   return ctx
   }

   export async function transcribe(path: string): Promise<string | null> {
   try {
   const whisper = await initTranscription()
   const { promise } = whisper.transcribe(path, {
   language: 'de',
   prompt: VOCABULARY,
   translate: false,
   maxThreads: 4,

   const { result } = await promise
   return result?.trim() ?? null
   } catch (e) {
   console.error('Transcription failed', e)
   return null
   } finally {
   // Delete the audio immediately and unconditionally.
   try { await RNFS.unlink(path) } catch {}
   }
   }

   export function useMessages(clientId: string) {
   const [messages, setMessages] = useState<any[]>([])
   useEffect(() => {
   supabase.from('messages').select('*')
   .eq('client_id', clientId)
   .is('acknowledged_at', null)
   .order('created_at', { ascending: false })
   .limit(5)
   .then(({ data }) => setMessages(data ?? []))

   const channel = supabase.channel('messages-' + clientId)
   .on('postgres_changes',
   { event: 'INSERT', schema: 'public', table: 'messages',
   filter: 'client_id=eq.' + clientId },
   ({ new: m }) => setMessages(prev => [m, ...prev]))
   .subscribe()

   return () => { supabase.removeChannel(channel) }
   }, [clientId])
   return messages
   }

CHAPTER 12

Beyond the Minimum Viable Product

The build specified in this document is the entry point, not the destination. This chapter sets out what comes next and
what each addition requires before it can be started.

12.1 Direction
The product's long-term position is the continuous record. Every existing product in this market serves one stage of
care and stops at its boundary. A person moving from independent living to home care to residential care currently
arrives at each stage with nothing, and the receiving organisation rebuilds the picture from scratch.

Aethon accumulates that picture from the first day a person joins, and carries it across every transition. The baseline,
the observation history, the care stage record and the person's own stated priorities travel with them. That record
becomes more valuable the longer it runs, which is the opposite of most software in this sector.

Three consequences follow from that position, and they set the priority order for everything below.

  •​ The record must never be locked to one organisation. A person changing service provider takes their record with
     them.
  •​ Longitudinal data accumulates value with time, so recording begins before there is any use for it. That is why the
     baseline and care stage exist in the minimum build.
  •​ The eventual commercial position rests on outcomes over time, not on features. Features are how the record gets
     populated.

  43.​     A single sentence of purpose. For example: Aethon is a care coordination and documentation tool

B.1 Production policies
Replace the development policies before any deployment with real people. The pattern below restricts each role to
the records it needs.

  Production policy pattern
  -- Drop the development policies first.
  -- A carer sees clients they have a visit note for, or all clients
  -- within their organisation once an organisation table exists.

  create policy carer_read_clients on clients for select
   using (
   exists (select 1 from user_profiles p
   where p.id = auth.uid() and p.role in ('carer','management'))
   );

  -- A resident or family member sees only their own client record.
  create policy own_client on clients for select
   using (
   exists (select 1 from user_profiles p
   where p.id = auth.uid() and p.client_id = clients.id)
   );

  -- Visit notes are readable by carers and management only.
  create policy carer_notes on visit_notes for all
   using (
   exists (select 1 from user_profiles p
   where p.id = auth.uid() and p.role in ('carer','management'))
   );

  -- Messages are readable by the client and by any user linked to them.
  create policy client_messages on messages for all
   using (
   exists (select 1 from user_profiles p
   where p.id = auth.uid() and p.client_id = messages.client_id)
   );
